# Physical Armour Boost (Up to 10)
## Przepisy
### DOS2 – Crafting Overhaul
[[Składniki/ARMOR BELT|Armor Belt]] + [[Składniki/LEATHER SCRAPS|Leather Scraps]] = [[Pancerze/PHYSICAL ARMOUR BOOST (UP TO 10)|Physical Armour Boost (Up to 10)]]
- **Uwagi:** Boost amount scales with level. Example: You can get 1-2 physical armor boost with 1 scrap material when you are level 3. You can get 10 physical armor boost when you are level 25. This is an exact value not decimal.
